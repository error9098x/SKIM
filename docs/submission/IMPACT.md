# Impact: the financing gap SKIM fills

Machines earn every day but can't borrow against that income. Every number is from a page we opened (12 Oct 2026).

**1. The borrowers exist.** peaq reports **3.3 million machine identities, 200 million+ transactions and 60+ applications** ([peaq, Economics 2.0, 30 Jul 2026](https://www.peaq.xyz/blog/introducing-peaq-economics-2-0-upgraded-to-set-machines-free)).

**2. Hardware is capital-heavy.** One public DC fast-charging connector costs **$38,000–$90,000** in hardware plus **$20,000–$60,000** to install ([US DOE AFDC](https://afdc.energy.gov/fuels/electricity-infrastructure-development)). Nearly **1.8 million** public charging points were added worldwide in 2025, past **7 million** in total ([IEA, Global EV Outlook 2026](https://www.iea.org/reports/global-ev-outlook-2026/electric-vehicle-charging-chap-6-and-10)).

**3. Operators avoid that capex.** The IFR president says companies rent or subscribe "to integrate automation without making a heavy upfront investment". The robot-as-a-service fleet grew **31%** in 2024 ([IFR, World Robotics 2025](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/service-robots-see-global-growth-boom)). In US transport and logistics robots, RaaS is now the dominant model ([IFR, World Robotics 2026](https://ifr.org/ifr-press-releases/news/global-sales-of-professional-service-robots-surge-24-percent)).

**4. DePIN names the problem.** About **$10B** circulating market cap, about **$72M** onchain revenue in FY25. To scale, DePINs "must either adopt InfraFi, focus on capex light infrastructure with fast paybacks, or take advantage of speculative capital in bull markets" ([Messari, State of DePIN 2025](https://messari.io/report/state-of-depin-2025)).

**5. peaq's rails start larger.** Initial Machine Offerings serve "medium-to-large robotics projects", and structured debt serves "large-scale deployment" ([peaq Machine Financing](https://www.peaq.xyz/machine-financing)). Economics 2.0 lists "rating-backed financing" as a Pro-tier function ([same post](https://www.peaq.xyz/blog/introducing-peaq-economics-2-0-upgraded-to-set-machines-free)).

**Where SKIM fits.** A credit line for one machine: its Machine NFT is collateral, a fixed share of every routed payment repays it, and repayment raises the rating that sizes the next line. It fills the space between one charger and an IMO. On agung testnet it has run end to end twice (0.1 then 0.2 AGNG); production sizing is untested.
